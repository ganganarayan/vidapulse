FAQ

Questions, answered

The wedge is the answer to most of these: we read your video, we don't host it.

Is VidaPulse really free?

Yes. VidaPulse is free forever on 1 video — no trial clock, no expiry, no credit card required. You only upgrade when you need more videos or deeper analytics like heatmaps, source attribution, and conversion tracking.

Do I have to move my video to VidaPulse?

No. VidaPulse isn't a video host. It reads the video you already have — YouTube, S3, Google Drive, or your own player — so there's no re-uploading, no re-embedding, and no risk to a funnel that already works.

How does it track without hosting my video?

You paste one lightweight script onto the page your video already sits on. It wraps your existing video, starts a viewer session, and streams play, drop-off, and re-watch events to VidaPulse in real time. The video never leaves where it lives.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It's one script tag on the page your video already sits on. It works on custom sites, ClickFunnels, GoHighLevel, WordPress, Webflow, and plain HTML.

How is VidaPulse different from Wistia, Vidyard, or Vidalytics?

Those are video hosts: their analytics require moving your video onto their player, and they bill by storage, bandwidth, or per seat. VidaPulse sits on top of your existing video and shows where buyers drop off — one flat price per account.

Is my viewers' data private?

Yes. All analytics data lives in your account, is never sold or shared, and the tracker identifies viewers by an anonymous ID — no personally identifiable information and no third-party cookies.

Who is VidaPulse for?

B2B SaaS teams, lead-gen and performance agencies, and high-ticket service firms running paid or owned traffic to a VSL, demo, or application video — anyone whose pipeline depends on a video converting.
